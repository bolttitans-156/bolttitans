BOLT TITANS · MICROBIAL FUEL CELL PROJECT

Meet Terra.

Terra is our researched microbial fuel cell (MFC) concept: bacteria living in soil and organic waste can break down biodegradable material and transfer electrons to an anode, creating an electrical current through an external circuit.

“From Waste to Watts.”RESEARCHED CONCEPT · MICROBIAL FUEL CELL
HOW TERRA WORKS

Waste goes in.
Electrons come out.

Our setup follows the core MFC architecture: an anode side where microbes oxidize organic material, a cathode side where the reduction reaction occurs, an ion pathway between them, and an external circuit that carries the electrons.

A question to explore

Anode: where the microbes work

The anode is the microbe-rich side of the cell. Bacteria living in the soil or organic-waste material break down biodegradable substrates and release electrons during their metabolism.

Research-backed concept

Terra is based on the established microbial fuel cell principle: microorganisms at the anode oxidize biodegradable substrates and transfer electrons to an electrode.

Our project focus is applying that principle to soil/organic waste in a compact, educational setup while measuring the electrical output rather than making untested performance claims.
