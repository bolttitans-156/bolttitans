Meet BioSpark.

What if yesterday's organic waste could help power tomorrow? We're exploring the science and possibilities behind turning organic material into usable electricity.

A peek at an idea we're still developing
THE QUESTIONS THAT SPARK IDEAS

Curiosity goes
around in circles.

There is plenty to figure out before a first build. Here are a few questions guiding our research.

A question to explore

How could waste make power?

One route we're researching is anaerobic digestion. Microorganisms break down organic material without oxygen, producing biogas that could potentially fuel a generator. We're still investigating whether this fits our project.

Still at the idea stage

BioSpark is not a built or tested system yet. The process, materials and expected energy output are all questions for research, not settled project results.

Our next step is to compare possible approaches and work out what a safe, small-scale demonstration could involve.
