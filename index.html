WHO WE ARE

Built to
compete.

Bolt Titans is our NRL 2026 robotics team — a group of students turning ideas, CAD models, electronics, code and countless prototypes into one competition-ready machine.

We believe the best robot isn't created in one night. It is engineered through testing, failure, iteration and a team that refuses to stop improving.

OUR MISSION

Engineer smarter. Build stronger. Compete together. Leave the arena better than we entered it.
